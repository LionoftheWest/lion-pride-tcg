Lion Pride TCG — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Lion Pride TCG (the "Game") is a free, hobby trading-card game that runs as a Discord bot and a Discord Activity. This policy explains what data the Game stores and why. The Game is a personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.

What the Game stores

  • Your Discord user ID, username, and display name — to identify you and to show who pulled or traded a card.
  • Your game data — your card collection, your pack balance, and a record of pack opens, gifts, and trades, with timestamps.

The Game does not collect your email, password, payment details, message content, or any Discord credential. It never asks for your password.

How the Game uses this data

  • To run the Game — show your collection, grant packs, and process gifts and trades.
  • To show a community feed of recent card pulls and to let players find each other for trades.

What other players can see

Gameplay is social. Your username and your recent pulls can appear to other players in the community feed, in trade offers, and in gift messages. This is a normal part of the Game.

Where the data lives

Game data is stored in Supabase (a hosted database service). Access is limited to the Game's own server processes. The Game does not sell your data and does not share it with third parties, except with the hosting service that stores it, or when required by law.

Data retention and your choices

Your data is kept while you play. You can ask for a copy of your data or ask to have it deleted. To make a request, contact the operator (see below). After deletion, your game progress is removed.

Age

The Game follows Discord's minimum age requirement. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

This policy may change. The effective date above shows the latest version.

Contact

For any privacy request — including a request to see or delete your data — send a direct message to the server owner on Discord.
