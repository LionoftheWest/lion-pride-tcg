Privacy Policy

Lion Pride TCG · Effective 29 September 2026

This policy explains what data Lion Pride TCG ("the game": the Discord Activity and the Discord bot) uses, why, and how you can have it removed. The owner of the Lion Pride Discord server runs the game.

1. Data the game uses

  • Your Discord account basics. When you open the Activity, Discord signs you in with the identify permission only. The game gets your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar. It does not get your email address, your password or your private messages.
  • How many messages you post. The bot counts how many messages you post in the server each day, because messages earn packs. It does not read or store what the messages say.
  • Server member information. To show names and to do the boons and pranks that members play on each other, the bot uses your nickname, your roles and whether you are in a voice channel. When an effect changes your nickname, the bot saves your old nickname so that it can put it back.
  • Voice presence in the Activity. When you open the Activity in a voice channel, the other members in that Activity can see that you are there. The game keeps this only in memory while the Activity is open.
  • Your game data. Your cards, packs and pack history, stars and stat points, trades and gifts, Hunt battles and damage, the cards that you and others play on each other, your achievements and your profile choices (for example your spotlight cards).
  • Technical data. The servers keep short technical logs (for example errors and request times) to keep the game working and safe.

2. Why the game uses it

Only to run the game: to sign you in, give you packs, show your collection and profile, run trades, the weekly raid boss (the Pride Hunt) and the boons and pranks, post game updates in the server, and keep the game fair and secure.

3. What the game does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data for advertising or marketing.
  • It has no ads, no payments and no third-party analytics or tracking.

4. Who else handles the data

  • Discord runs the platform. Its own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Discord.
  • Supabase stores the game database (in the United States).
  • A cloud server in the United States runs the Activity and the bot.

Some game information is visible to other members of the server by design: for example your name in feeds and leaderboards, your profile and collection, Hunt standings, and the cards played on you.

5. How long the game keeps data

Your game data is kept while the game runs, so that you keep your collection. Temporary data (for example active effects and voice presence) ends by itself. Technical logs are kept only for a short time.

6. Your choices

  • You can stop playing at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your game data, or for your game data to be deleted. Contact the server owner on Discord (lionofthewest94). The data is deleted within 30 days. A deletion removes your collection and cannot be undone.

7. Children

The game is for members of a Discord server, so you must meet Discord's minimum age for your country.

8. Security

Access to the database is limited to the game's servers, every request is checked against your Discord sign-in, and secrets are not stored in the game's public code. No system is perfectly secure, but the game takes reasonable steps to protect your data.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy can change. The new version is posted on this page with a new date.

10. Contact

Contact the owner of the Lion Pride Discord server (lionofthewest94) on Discord.
