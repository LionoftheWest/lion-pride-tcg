## FAQ (the ? button)

**How do I earn packs?**
Play! Your first message of the day earns 1 pack, and 25 messages earn 1 more. The Dailies add more: check in, use 8 cards on the Raid Boss, spend 30 minutes in voice with someone, and trade or play a boon. You can earn up to 5 of these "activity" packs a day. Gifts, Raid Boss rewards and achievements are extra and do not count toward the 5.

**When does the day reset?**
At midnight Mountain Time (MT).

**What are the rarities?**
Normal, Illustrated Rare, Secret Rare, Full Art and Gold. Per card: Normal 93.8%, Illustrated Rare 5%, Secret Rare 0.6%, Full Art 0.4%, Gold 0.2%.

**What do duplicates do?**
A duplicate adds a star to your card, up to 5 stars. Each star gives 3 stat points: Attack, Vitality, Precision, Potency and Haste. You can reset your points for free once a week (Monday, MT).

**When is the Raid Boss?**
A new boss appears every Thursday at 3 PM MT and stays until Monday at 5 PM MT. You can attack with up to 8 cards a day. When the boss falls, the hunters share a pack reward by the damage they dealt. If it escapes, there is a smaller reward.

**How do Dailies work?**
Open the calendar button. Each daily you finish shows a Claim button, and the red number shows how many you can claim. Check in every day to build a streak: day 3 and day 7 give a bonus pack.

**How do boons and pranks work?**
Cards with an effect can be played on another member. Boons help (for example a spotlight role or a gift pack). Pranks are for fun (a nickname, a color, a mustache, a short timeout). You can receive at most 5 pranks a day and at most 2 timeouts a day. Some cards block, reflect or redirect pranks, and a cleanse removes them.

**How do trades work?**
In Community, pick a member, pick your card and theirs, and send the offer. Swaps are the same rarity. You can also gift a card or a pack.

**How do I stop pings or "is playing" posts?**
Open the bell, then Settings. Turn off any ping you do not want, or turn off "Show when I play".

**How do I replay the tutorial?**
Tap the ? button, then Replay tutorial.
