# Balance numbers

> Generated by `card-studio/scripts/gen-data-docs.mjs` from the database catalog. Do not edit this file by hand: change the
> `COMMENT ON` in a migration, apply it, and run the generator again.

Every number that changes card power, combat or rewards is in the `balance` table (one row per key). The SQL functions read it with
`balance_get` and `balance_num`. The Activity and the bot read it with a 60 s cache. Each change writes a `balance_log` row.
The values below are the values of the database that made this page.

## The rules for a change

The triggers on `balance` check each change:

- `balance_check` (`balance_check()`): Trigger (before insert, update, delete on balance): refuses a delete, a negative number, an update that removes a leaf or changes its type, and a bad stars row. Sets updated_at and updated_by.
- `balance_check_ability_v2` (`balance_check_ability_v2()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): the key ability_v2 must match its fixed shape and limits (ability_v2_spec, walked by balance_shape_errors) and words_min must not be above words_max. Every other key passes. combat_v2_abilities.sql.
- `balance_check_boss_counters` (`balance_check_boss_counters()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): boss_counters must hold only numbers; share, boss_share and the move shares (heal, shield, empower, expose, smite, share, keep, curse, back) from 0 to 1; rounds, wait, plays, cards and hits whole numbers (plays and hits at least 1); hit sizes and multipliers above 0; the weights add up to more than 0. boss_passives: the counter passive numbers (plague_x, shatterer_x, dispeller_x, juggernaut_x, juggernaut_stun_fail, plague_cleanse) from 0 to 1.
- `balance_check_combat` (`balance_check_combat()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): combat.element_bonus must be a number from 1 to 2, combat.element_penalty a number from 0.5 to 1 and combat.auto_elements true or false (f3_element_bonus.sql, f3_auto_elements.sql). A key that is absent is not checked.
- `balance_check_combat_v2` (`balance_check_combat_v2()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): every combat v2 key must match its fixed shape and limits (balance_v2_spec): no missing, extra or wrong leaf. A key named v2_* with no shape is refused. Extra rules: v2_asc (the curve from 0 to 1, never falling; base not above top; every card rarity has a base or an alias), v2_enemy (the boss move chances add up to 1). The limits of v2_speed keep status_min_x at or below 1 and safety_max_x at or above 1. combat_v2_flags_balance.sql.
- `balance_check_dungeon` (`balance_check_dungeon()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): dungeon_rewards tier_weights, chest.shards, chest.card_chance and offers heal / buff / shards / ward / revive must be 5 numbers (tier 1 to 5), tier_weights must add up to more than 0, the Shards whole numbers, chest.card_chance and door.gamble_rare at most 1, door.rare_tier and offers.min_tier whole numbers from 1 to 5. (balance_check keeps every leaf and its type, so boss_hp_estimate keeps its 4 numbers.)
- `balance_check_economy` (`balance_check_economy()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): the pulls rates must add up to 1, pulls pack_size must be a whole number from 1 to 20, daily streak_cycle must be at least 1.
- `balance_check_enemy_v2` (`balance_check_enemy_v2()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): the key enemy_v2 must match its fixed shape and limits (enemy_v2_spec, walked by balance_shape_errors) and rules.brood.start must not be above rules.brood.max. Every other key passes. combat_v2_monsters.sql.
- `balance_check_report_shots` (`balance_check_report_shots()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): the shape of the key report_shots (report_shots.sql): keep_days, per_report and max_bytes whole numbers of at least 1, per_report at most 9, max_bytes at most 5242880 (the Activity reads at most 5 MB of an upload).
- `balance_check_runs` (`balance_check_runs()`): Trigger (before insert or update on balance): dungeon_runs_per_day and gauntlet_runs_per_day must be whole numbers from 1 to 10.
- `balance_check_settings` (`balance_check_settings()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): the shapes of the keys dungeon, gauntlet and adventure_gate (balance_settings_numbers.sql) and dungeon_rewards.chest_rarity. dungeon: squad, budget, floors, round_cap and rules.budget whole numbers of at least 1; a cost for every card rarity; hp_growth, atk_growth, foe_mult hp / atk above 0; rest_heal and rest_revive shares from 0 to 1; fight_min and horde_min whole numbers 1 to 8; fight_spread and horde_spread whole numbers 1 to 4; early_floors a whole number 0 to 30; early_hp and early_atk from 0.1 to 1; foe_heal_cap from 0 to 5; room_weights numbers that add up to more than 0. gauntlet: budget a whole number of at least 1; room_weights the same, without treasure. adventure_gate: attackers a whole number. dungeon_rewards.chest_rarity: tiers 1 to 5, each 3 numbers that add up to more than 0.
- `balance_check_stat_labels` (`balance_check_stat_labels()`): Trigger (before insert, update on balance): stat_labels must have the five stats, each with a label (1 to 12 characters), a short (1 to 4) and a text with {n} exactly once (f3_stat_labels.sql).
- `balance_log_write` (`balance_log_write()`): Trigger (after insert, update on balance): writes a balance_log row with the old value, the new value and balance_who. Writes nothing when the value and the note did not change.

- The shape of a value stays the same: an update cannot remove a leaf or change its type (see `balance_check`).

### ability_v2

Combat v2: the ability rules of the validator and the card text (plan 5; combat_v2_abilities.sql). pp = the price model of the ability blocks beside v2_pp: an attacker block costs att_k / C x the extra basic hits it adds (C = v2_charge.<class> + 1 own turns a cycle); a support block costs its v2_pp.support_pp price x sup_cycle / C; a support "x damage vs a status" costs sup_vs_k x sup_cycle / C x (x - 1) x the frozen share x the payoff share; each status of an execute list costs line_status; quicken on every attacker costs quicken_all_x times. silver = the Silver upgrade (plan 5.3): at stars stars the active gets pp more PP on one amount (type 1). limits = blocks (the most blocks of one active, the engine step limit), riders (at most this many riders, plan 5.3), words_min to words_max words of card text (Nathan 2026-10-20, the card text style; replaces 8 to 14). k1_shield = the shield of the cured ally that the Glossary keyword "Cure ... for N steps" names. Read by ability_v2_check, the studio builder (T1) and the simulator (config.json ability_v2); no fight reads it. Shape: ability_v2_spec (trigger balance_check_ability_v2). share (PR T1, Nathan 2026-10-20 decisions 1 and 2; measured by card-studio/v2sim/run.mjs shares, 2000 runs, seed 1) = the share of the actives where a condition is true: a gate (self_has, enemy_charging, foes_alive, self_shield) costs its host x (share - 1), so the pair costs the host x the share; on_kill = an attacker active downs its target while another enemy stands (the trigger costs like on_crit with this share). ref.per_ally = the mean number of other standing attackers at an attacker active (the scaler per_ally). PR T1b (Nathan 2026-10-20, the 6 approved recommendations; measured by card-studio/v2sim/run.mjs shares, 2000 runs, seed 1): pp.vs_smite = the measured raw value of x times the damage on a support smite, per smite price x the smite x (x - 1) (no T1b function reads pp.sup_vs_k, a fixed smite size: it stays, a balance leaf is never removed); share.self_has_each = one share for each card status (the share of the actives where this card has it; no T1b function reads the one share share.self_has, which stays); share.area_payoff = the share of the other enemies with a status / the share of the main targets with it, at the area actives (a payoff counts on each area hit); ref.list_extra = the share that each added status of a list adds to the share of its condition (at most 1). Minimum share (Nathan 2026-10-20, approved after PR T1b; combat_v2_min_share.sql): limits.min_share = the validator refuses a condition whose measured share (share.self_has_each.<status> of a self_has, share.enemy_charging, share.foes_alive, share.self_shield, share.on_kill) is below this number: such a block costs almost 0 PP and almost never works. The limits 0 to 0.2: 0 turns the rule off. Two leaves are retired but kept (no function reads them; a balance leaf is never removed until the balance guard can retire one): pp.sup_vs_k (since T1b: pp.vs_smite) and share.self_has (since T1b: share.self_has_each).

<a id="key-autopick"></a>

### autopick

The Hunt auto-pick (hunt_autopick). Two supports: the second one counts x repeat_x when it has the same effect as the first, x same_role_x when both keep the squad alive (sustain) or both add damage; effect_x weighs an effect (cleanse helps only against a debuff). candidates: at most this many attackers enter the search (the strongest by their best possible value; the cards that fought today always enter). The fight numbers (synergy, weakness, armored, affinity) come from combat, boss_passives and support.

Shape: `object { candidates: number, effect_x: object { cleanse: number }, repeat_x: number, same_role_x: number, sustain: array(3) of string }`

```json
{
  "sustain": [
    "heal",
    "shield",
    "weaken"
  ],
  "effect_x": {
    "cleanse": 0.6
  },
  "repeat_x": 0.5,
  "candidates": 30,
  "same_role_x": 0.85
}
```

### boss_counters

The Hunt boss counter moves (the boss pools are content in settings.hunt_boss_moves: counters, key, name, text). share = the share of the normal boss turns that a counter move takes (boss_share = a boss's own share); weights = the draw weight of each move (hunt_counter_pick). moves = the numbers of each move (hunt_counter_act, hunt_attack, hunt_support): hit_x = ATK multiplier of one hit (combat_area_roll), heal_x / weaken_x / support_x / times = the hit on that card type x this, hits = the number of hits; heal / shield / empower / expose / smite = that support works at this share for the rest of the day; wait = the extra rounds of the support cooldown; rounds = the rounds of a damage-over-time (dot_x = ATK multiplier per round) or an enrage (enrage = the boss damage x this); share = Crush: the share of the lost shield; keep = Rattle: the share of each shield that stays; curse = the damage x of a cursed card; back = Demotion: the share of the damage that comes back; x = Counter-pick: the bonus x this hurts the attacker; step = Rage Spiral: the boss damage added by each weaken; plays = the support plays that do nothing; cards = Ban: the support cards that stop. The move texts quote these numbers (hunt_boss_move_list). balance_check_boss_counters checks the shape.

### combat

A card hit (hunt_attack). Chances: miss, block, crit (crit_weak on a weak tag; Precision adds, under stat_points.crit_cap). Damage = combat power x weakness x (roll_min + random x roll_span) x buffs x synergy x passives; crit x crit_x, block x block_x. Weakness = 1 + (1 - weak_step^weak hits) (halved per weak-tag card after weak_stack_free) - resist_step x (1 - resist_decay^resists), between weak_min and weak_max. Element chart (f3_element_bonus.sql, rule A): element_bonus = the damage multiplier when the attacker's element beats the target's element (element_counters, approved pairs only; from 1 to 2; 1 = no bonus). element_penalty = the multiplier when the target's element beats the attacker's element (from 0.5 to 1; 1 = no penalty). An enabler of a rarity in enabler_extra_stack applies one more stack (a proposal). area_one_foe_x: a support area smite hits the target and its area value hits every other foe; with one foe (the Hunt) it adds area_one_foe_x of the area value to the smite (P3, Combat Lab 2026-10-19, approved; the same rule as v2_roles.cleaver.hunt_area_x). Nothing reads this key until combat_v2 is on.

Shape: `object { area_one_foe_x: number, basic_x: number, enabler_extra_stack: array(3) of string, power_exp: number, power_ref: number }`

```json
{
  "basic_x": 0.18,
  "power_exp": 0.1,
  "power_ref": 25,
  "area_one_foe_x": 0.2,
  "enabler_extra_stack": [
    "full_art",
    "event",
    "gold"
  ]
}
```
